# ClipCheck 0.5.1

- **New: built-in video editor.** Open any clip from the gallery or player in the editor to trim, cut and rearrange it without leaving ClipCheck. Multi-track timeline with separate lanes for video and each audio stream (game, microphone, push to talk), waveforms and thumbnails, and linked audio that stays in sync with its video.
- **Editing tools:** Cut tool with a snapped preview line (hold Shift to turn snapping off), easy edge trimming, move, duplicate, copy/paste, join, speed changes, reverse, fades and transitions. Edits that overlap overwrite what is underneath, and closing a gap ripples all unlocked tracks. Gaps can be selected and deleted.
- **Timeline navigation:** zoom from a few seconds up to a 4 hour project on one screen, a horizontal scrollbar and zoom slider, and scrubbing from the ruler.
- **Live preview:** play every audio stream together with the video, with smooth scrubbing. A diagnostics overlay is available with Ctrl+Alt+D.
- **Export:** choose resolution and frame rate, codec and encoder. Hardware encoders are listed by their real GPU names, and Auto picks the GPU with the most video memory (your discrete card rather than the integrated one). Bitrate is capped at 200 Mbps and retried automatically if audio would not fit.
- **Settings → Video editor:** export presets and encoders, preview quality, and the editor cache (see its size, clear it, or set a limit).
- Fixes: "Show in folder" now selects the file in Explorer, and MP4 remux no longer writes oversized files or makes export slow to start.
- Known limitation: preview playback can feel less smooth on some machines. Exports are not affected.
